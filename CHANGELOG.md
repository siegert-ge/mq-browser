# Changelog

## 1.0.0 – 2026-10

First public release.

- Browse-only access to IBM MQ queues on several queue managers at once
- Time-range filter, full-text search (AND, phrases, exclusions, regex), histogram
- Decoding of MQMD, RFH2, embedded Hex/Base64, dead-letter (MQDLH), transmission (MQXQH) and PCF messages
- Fixed-length and delimited records with record layouts, EBCDIC, zoned and packed decimals
- Header mode and early stop for deep queues
- Routes (saved queue filters), export to text, open in editor
- Trial edition: 14 days full, then Lite (10 messages per browse, no export)
